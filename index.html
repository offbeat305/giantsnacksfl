About

Snacks are all we do. Since 2012.

From our warehouse in Deerfield Beach we deliver the brands shoppers trust to stores in Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, Martin, St. Lucie and Glades counties.

What we do

A wholesaler that knows the snack aisle

Giant Snacks supplies salty snacks, dips, variety packs and beverages to grocery stores, convenience stores, gas stations, markets and vending operators. Our list covers potato chips, cheese puffs and curls, tortilla and corn chips, popcorn, onion rings, pork rinds, plantain and yuca strips, salsa and dips, and ready-to-drink sodas, energy drinks and chocolate milk.

Because snacks are all we do, we keep the sizes retailers actually need: small bags for the counter and vending, mid-size bags for grab-and-go, larger bags for the grocery aisle, and multi-packs for family shoppers.
